--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Layer Stackup: 2 Copper Layers (Double-Sided PCB)
  * Top Layer (F.Cu): Red traces, component mounting pads, solid ground copper fill zone.
  * Bottom Layer (B.Cu): Blue traces providing cross-under routing and reference return paths.
- Component Mounting Strategy: Single-Sided Top Placement
  * All surface-mount (SMD) and through-hole (THT) components are mounted on the top layer.
  * Bottom side contains only through-hole solder joints, pin tails, and routing tracks.
- Board Substrate: Standard FR-4 Glass Epoxy, 1.6 mm nominal thickness.
- Solder Mask: Standard Green Photoimageable Solder Mask (LPI) on both sides.
- Silkscreen: White legend on top side (F.SilkS).
- Interconnect Technology: Plated Through-Hole (PTH) annular vias connecting F.Cu and B.Cu.
- I/O Termination: Three 9-pin headers providing direct breakout for Port 0, Port 1, and Port 2, each paired with a dedicated +5V power rail pin.


4. FORENSIC ENGINEERING AUDIT & DESIGN OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Schematic Net Naming & Power Flow:
   - In Eeschema, the net preceding diode D1 is labeled "+5V", and the output of U1 (pin 3) is also labeled "+5V". Because power port symbols in KiCad are global nets, this establishes an unintended short between the regulator's input and output nets.
   - For a standard 7805 linear regulator, a minimum dropout voltage of ~2.0V requires an input of >= 7.0V DC. If an external 5V supply is connected to the input, the regulator will fall out of regulation (supplying only ~3.6V due to diode drop and internal pass-transistor drop).
   - Recommendation: Label the input net as "VIN" or "VEXT" (7V to 12V DC input) and retain "+5V" strictly for the regulated output bus.

B. Reset Polarity Architecture:
   - The microcontroller used is the Silicon Labs C8051F382-GQ, which features an active-low reset pin labeled "~RST/C2CK" (Pin 13).
   - In the schematic, the reset circuit is configured with C3 connected to +5V and R7 connected to GND. In classic 8051 architecture (e.g. AT89S52), reset is active-high, where this RC topology works. However, on an active-low ~RST pin, tying a pull-down resistor to GND will pull the reset pin LOW permanently, keeping the MCU in continuous hardware reset!
   - Recommendation: Connect R7 as a pull-up resistor to +5V (typically 1kΩ - 4.7kΩ as specified in the Silicon Labs datasheet) and place the capacitor C3 or pushbutton SW1 to GND to pull the pin LOW upon manual press or power-up.

C. Microcontroller Power & REGIN Pin:
   - On the C8051F382, Pin 10 is VDD and Pin 11 is REGIN (the on-chip 5V-to-3.3V voltage regulator input).
   - The schematic shows Pin 11 (REGIN) marked with a no-connect flag, while Pin 10 (VDD) is connected to +5V. For Silicon Labs C8051 devices operating from a 5V supply, the 5V power must be fed to REGIN (Pin 11), and VDD (Pin 10) serves as the 3.3V regulated core output requiring an external decoupling capacitor.

D. Silkscreen Text Consolidation:
   - In front_layout.jpeg, reference designator silkscreen labels are consolidated into dense columnar text blocks along the left, right, and bottom board perimeters.
   - While readable during assembly if cross-referenced, best practices require individual reference designators to be situated directly adjacent to their respective component footprint silkscreen boundaries.

E. DRC Evidence Status:
   - No DRC report files (.rpt) or dialog screenshots exist in the project directory. Consequently, verification status cannot be certified as DRC-passed. Clearances around the 0.5 mm pitch TQFP-48 pads and silkscreen over copper pours must be verified against manufacturer design rules (e.g., 6/6 mil trace/space).
