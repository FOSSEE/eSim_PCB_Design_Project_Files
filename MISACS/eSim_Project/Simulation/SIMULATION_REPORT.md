## 2. Model Fidelity & Validation Boundary Declarations

1. **TB1 (24V Protection):** Static DC sweep (Rsrc=0.5 ohm); models DC reverse blocking of D2, static forward clamp of D14 reverse-polarity shunt diode, and DC TVS clamping of SMBJ30A. Verifies static protection behaviour under its stated model, but does not prove dynamic reverse-polarity fuse coordination. D14 survival under actual installation fault conditions remains unverified. Engineering qualification: CONDITIONAL / NOT VERIFIED.
2. **TB2 (LM2576 Buck):** Open-loop 52kHz pulse switch with Bourns SRR1260-101M worst-case DCR (180mR). Does not model closed-loop LM2576 error amplifier dynamics, loop compensation, or line/load step transient response.
3. **TB3 (AMS1117-3.3 LDO):** Behavioural dropout model with physical series PCB trace resistance (18mR) and inductance (16nH). Demonstrates expected 5.14mV DC drop at 285.3mA peak load. The simulation verifies the behavioural LDO's DC dropout response and distributed PCB trace drop. It does not establish AMS1117 AC loop stability, phase margin, or stability under dynamic ESP32 Wi-Fi pulsed loads. Physical prototype testing is required. Engineering qualification: NOT VERIFIED.
4. **TB4 & TB5 (Analog Front Ends):** Precision shunt (100.0R) and divider (4:1) with behavioural VCVS clipping buffers. Model DC transfer linearity; do not simulate physical LMV358 op-amp silicon parasitics.
5. **TB6 (ADC Protection):** Generic parameterized Schottky diode macro with 1.0k series limiting resistor under nominal room-temperature (25°C) static DC sweep with fixed 3.30V rail. Does not utilize a vendor-qualified worst-case BAT54S model, does not model dynamic supply rail rise, does not model sub-zero VF temperature derating, and does not simulate ADS1115 internal ESD protection diode structures. Engineering qualification: CONDITIONAL / NOT VERIFIED.
6. **TB7, TB8 & TB9 (Power MOSFET Stage):** Generic Level 1 NMOS models with lumped capacitances and ideal pulse drives. Model basic RC gate charging, dual resistive-load switching, and SS56 catch diode flyback clamping. Do not evaluate board crosstalk, cable parasitics, thermal coupling, or shoot-through.
7. **TB10 (RS-485 Bus):** Ideal pulse source stimulus into 60 ohm terminated line with 390 ohm fail-safe bias network. Models differential bus swing and DC bias; does not model physical MAX3485E transceiver driver silicon.
